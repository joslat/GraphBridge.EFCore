# Larkfield library opens a seed bank

By Dana Whitfield

*A fictional article written for the GraphBridge samples.*

The Larkfield Public Library opened a community seed bank on Saturday, lending vegetable and flower seeds the way it lends books. Head librarian Tomas Reyes cut the ribbon in the library's reading room on Elm Street.

“Seeds are stories we can plant,” Reyes said. “Borrow a packet in spring, and bring some seeds back after the harvest.”

The seed bank is part of the library's sustainability program, funded by the Larkfield City Council. Volunteers from the Larkfield Garden Club sorted more than two thousand packets before the opening.
