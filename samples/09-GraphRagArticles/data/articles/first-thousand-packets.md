# Seed bank lends its first thousand packets

By Dana Whitfield

*A fictional article written for the GraphBridge samples.*

Three months after it opened, the seed bank at the Larkfield Public Library has lent its first thousand packets. Tomatoes and sunflowers were the most popular, according to head librarian Tomas Reyes.

“People come back with jars of seeds and photos of their gardens,” Reyes said.

The Larkfield Garden Club will hold its spring planting day in Mill Park on June 14, with seedlings grown from the library's own seeds. Club president Amara Chen said the event is open to everyone.
