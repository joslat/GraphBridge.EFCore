# Capability and evidence map

GraphBridge is an experimental non-relational EF Core provider for a **documented subset** of Neo4j workloads. This page maps useful scenarios to contracts and recorded verification. Historical results apply to their stated revisions; they are not a fresh release qualification of the current tree. The repository's acceptance suites are maintainer-local; their named cases below identify the evidence without implying they ship in a public clone.

| Scenario | Contract and executable example | Fixture / named acceptance | Recorded evidence | Known exclusion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EF LINQ filters, parameters and entity identity | [Compatibility](../../docs/reference/COMPATIBILITY.md), [inspection](../../docs/features/QUERY_INSPECTION.md), [Blogging](../../samples/01-Blogging-CodeFirst/README.md) | Owned Community fixture; `ProviderQueryTests.Where_uses_server_parameters_and_reuses_shape_with_changed_closure_values` and `Tracked_query_uses_EF_identity_and_preserves_original_values` | [Baseline and later checkpoints](VERIFICATION_SUMMARY.md) | No general joins/GroupBy or client predicate fallback; async execution |
| Tracked writes, concurrency and rollback | [Recovery contract](../../docs/features/TRANSACTIONS_AND_CONCURRENCY.md), [Blogging](../../samples/01-Blogging-CodeFirst/README.md) | Owned fixture; `ProviderUpdateTests.Two_write_constraint_failure_is_atomic_and_preserves_Added_EF_states` and `Concurrent_two_context_updates_have_exactly_one_rowversion_winner` | [Recorded provider live gates](VERIFICATION_SUMMARY.md) | `SaveChangesAsync` only; no transparent retry after uncertain commit, no cluster-wide exactly-once claim |
| Typed graph patterns and identified edge payloads | [Fluent contract](../../docs/features/FLUENT_CYPHER.md), [compiled movie factories](../../samples/05-FluentCypher/FluentMovieQueries.cs) | Synthetic movie graph with parallel credits; `ProviderFluentCypherTests.C1_coactors_distinct_rows_and_relationship_reuse_match_native_clauses` and `C2_edge_identity_payload_parameters_and_parallel_edges_match_native` | [C1/C2 and reviewed acceptance](FLUENT_CYPHER_ACCEPTANCE.md) | Read-only native semantics, detached DTOs; no tracked graph returns or write DSL |

| Ontology-first and database-first authoring | [Organization](../../samples/02-Organization-OntologyFirst/README.md), [Movies](../../samples/03-Movies-DatabaseFirst/README.md), [scaffold review](../../docs/tools/GRAPHBRIDGE_TOOL.md) | Explicit vocabulary/policy and Movies snapshot; `ScaffoldReviewTests.Equivalent_reordered_observations_preserve_report_and_compiled_model` and `Missing_constraint_preserves_reviewable_errors_without_claiming_model_equivalence` | [Tooling closure checkpoint](VERIFICATION_SUMMARY.md) | Optional authoring tools; no OWL inference, automatic schema deployment or inferred identity guarantees |

The published [version/profile matrix](VERSION_CAPABILITY_MATRIX.md) distinguishes tested profiles from unsupported or unverified ones.
